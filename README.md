# OSUN-STATE-2026-GOVERNORSHIP-ELECTION
Overview of the Osun State 2026 Governorship Election Dashboard. This is a Power BI-style election dashboard summarizing the just-concluded Osun State 2026 governorship election. It presents aggregate results, party performance, and a detailed breakdown of votes by local government area (LGA).

## Key headline figures:
- Total votes: 973,000
- Average votes per LGA: 32,000
- Party results:
 - ACCORD: 511,000 votes (52%)
 - APC: 445,000 votes (46%)
 - ADC: 17,000 votes (1.8%)

The dashboard includes candidate photos aligned with colored bars (yellow for ACCORD, blue for APC, green for ADC) and a horizontal bar chart ranking all LGAs by total votes cast.

## Analysis

### 1. Overall Outcome  
ACCORD secured a clear but relatively narrow victory with a 6-percentage-point lead over APC (52% vs 46%). ADC finished a distant third with under 2% of the vote, indicating a predominantly two-party contest. The total of 973K votes suggests a solid turnout relative to Osun’s registered voter base in recent cycles.

- Osogbo (the state capital) is the clear vote-heavyweight, contributing significantly more than any other LGA.
- Urban and semi-urban centres (Osogbo, Olorunda, Iwo, the Ife LGAs, Ede North) dominate the upper end of the ranking.
- Several smaller or more rural LGAs (Ifedayo, Boluwaduro, Atakunmosa East/West) contributed the fewest votes.

This distribution is typical for Osun: the capital and major commercial/educational centres drive overall turnout and volume.

### 3. Implications of the Results
- ACCORD’s win appears broad-based rather than heavily concentrated in one or two strongholds, given the relatively even spread of total votes across many LGAs. A 52–46 margin suggests competitive performance in both urban and rural areas.
- APC’s strong showing (only 66K votes behind) indicates it remained highly competitive and likely retained significant support in traditional strongholds.
- ADC’s minimal share confirms it was not a serious contender in this election.
